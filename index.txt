9:T819,I am an incoming postdoctoral researcher at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I successfully defended my PhD thesis in Computer Science and Engineering on July 27, 2026. I conducted my doctoral research at the [SmartX Lab](https://hkustsmartlab.github.io/) under the supervision of [Prof. Hao Chen](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/about/people/faculty/hao-chen). Prior to this, I obtained my BSc degree in Applied Mathematics from Harbin Institute of Technology (Weihai).

My research centers on building generalizable AI systems for computational pathology and biomedical intelligence, with a focus on large vision, vision-language, and multi-modal models that work reliably across diverse scientific and clinical settings. Specific directions include:

- **Pathology foundation models**: building clinically validated and generalizable pretrained models for whole-slide image analysis and lung pathology interpretation ([GPFM](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41551-025-01488-4), Nature Biomedical Engineering; [PulmoFoundation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25878), Under Review)
- **Multi-instance learning**: developing models that learn from collections of instances, identify task-relevant evidence, and produce robust slide-level / patient-level predictions under weak supervision for pathology diagnosis ([FOCUS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Guo_FOCUS_Knowledge-enhanced_Adaptive_Visual_Compression_for_Few-shot_Whole_Slide_Image_CVPR_2025_paper.html), CVPR 2025; [Querent](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/guo25j.html), ICML 2025)
- **Multi-modal learning**: developing frameworks that align heterogeneous signals and transfer knowledge across modalities, with applications in computational pathology and computational neuroscience ([HistGen](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/387-Paper0796.html), MICCAI 2024; [BLEND](https://openreview.net/forum?id=jE5ZbtMtcU), ICLR 2025)

In the meantime, I actively collaborate with clinicians and pathologists to translate developed models into real-world diagnostic workflows.
